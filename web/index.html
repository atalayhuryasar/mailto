💡 Note on First Launch (macOS Security)

As a community open-source project without a $99/yr Apple certificate, macOS may ask for first-time permission:

  1. Unzip and drag mailto.app into /Applications.
  2. If prompted on first click, click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Or run xattr -cr /Applications/mailto.app in Terminal to unlock instantly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does mailto: consume 0% idle CPU and RAM?
Unlike traditional apps that stay running in your dock or menu bar, mailto: configures itself solely as a LaunchServices URL handler with LSUIElement=true. When you click a mailto link, macOS wakes the process, mailto: parses the link and dispatches it according to your rules in under 15ms, and then terminates immediately.
How does the Fn key override work?
When a link is clicked, mailto: inspects the system hardware flags for the Fn (Globe) key. If you are holding Fn during the click, it bypasses all domain rules and immediately routes to your pre-configured Alternative Target (e.g. Apple Mail or Clipboard).
Are in-browser Chrome and Safari extensions planned?
Yes! Native Manifest V3 extensions for Chrome and Safari are currently on our roadmap to allow in-tab intercepting for users who prefer in-browser handling.
Does mailto: collect any analytics or email addresses?
Never. mailto: has no analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, and does not make any external network requests other than directing your browser to the webmail URL you configured. All settings are stored locally in your Mac's UserDefaults.
